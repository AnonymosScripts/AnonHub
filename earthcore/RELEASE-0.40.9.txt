ANONHUB 0.40.9 - INVENTORY MEDKIT AND ESP FILTERS

Auto Medkit now checks native HotbarController.Tools and CarryInventoryService
GetInventory(). A stored medkit is moved to Hotbar via its actual item ID,
equipped, consumed by the native tool, then the previous weapon is restored.
No manual equipment is required for this flow; the game must accept the move
and equipment. Full hotbar, driving restrictions and server healing time still
apply. No fabricated tool or direct health modification is used.
Preparation runs asynchronously, guards cancellation before mutation/equipment,
and pauses smart weapon swaps and new mining/armor jobs while healing.

ESP options: hide ShopItem-tagged items (including tagged ancestors), and hide
all bases/structures independently of visited-base filtering. PT/EN retained.

Not implemented: extending DrillDefense contact damage to cabin metal plates.
Captured data describes native drill contact damage; it contains no verified
client operation for assigning that damage to other objects. No dummy damage
toggle or local-only damage attribute is exposed.

Validation: medkit inventory/hotbar/backpack and cancellation simulations;
UI/utility/logistics checks; Luau compilation; protected release instruction
and string equivalence. Not validated live in Roblox/Delta.
