ANONHUB 0.40.10 - STAGING, MINESHAFT AND SHORTCUT IMAGES

Shop staging fills the floor before stacking identical footprints/groups.
Stacks are limited to three items and a conservative height/footprint ratio.
Checks real support floor and requires an empty purchase square before starting.
Adds clearance, clears residual assembly velocity while dragging, waits after
release and checks both the new placement and previous stacks for displacement.
Stops on drift instead of continuing to destabilize the batch. No anchoring or
automatic purchase is performed; fewer items may fit under the stability limits.

Auto Mine supports the active Mineshaft only while inside it. Outdoors it
excludes Dungeon-owned ores. Filters both ore targets and loose fragments by
root/ID; ignores ambiguous interior loot. Scope changes interrupt the operation.
Inside returns to the starting point; outside returns to the loaded drill.

Floating action buttons use image assets from the captured upgrade icon catalog.
Labels, outlines, independent visibility, drag handles and PT/EN are retained.

Validation: floor-first packing, stack height/count limits, Dungeon scope cases,
UI/utilities/medkit regressions, Luau compilation and protected build equivalence.
No live Roblox/Delta physics or mining validation was available.
