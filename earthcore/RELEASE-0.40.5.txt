ANONHUB 0.40.5 — DUNGEON LOOT, DEAD ESP, SHOP STAGING

Dead-enemy ESP now recognizes corpse category and corpse metadata even after
Humanoid removal or conversion into Workspace.Items. Hide dead enemies must be
enabled; living NPCs and player markers keep their separate behavior.

Dungeon collector resolves the native dungeon root, with active ID fallback.
Items without dungeon IDs can match the loaded dungeon bounding volume; explicit
conflicting IDs remain excluded. Exit readiness is checked when depositing,
not before collecting. Attempts to equip an available Sack; does not require an
empty sack. Waits for delayed loot replication, continues after failed pickups
and reports unconfirmed counts. Exit locks preserve the bag and halt transport.
Scope remains loaded, eligible Sack loot and enabled chest prompts. It does not
bypass boss locks or claim to collect every special currency/relic automatically.

New PT/EN Shop tab: load nearby shop stock, native item icons, names, estimated
prices, checkboxes and selection total. CONFIRM stages selected items in the
BuyPart volume through native drag requests. Packing is checked before moving;
oversized batches are rejected. No ShopService.Buy call, no Robux flow, and no
purchase is performed. Player checks the final register price and buys manually.
Physical replication and inventory layout still need live-game validation.

Validation: regression tests for corpses without Humanoids, dungeon ownership
and spatial fallback, cart bounds/overflow, PT/EN UI and stop/cleanup guards;
Luau compilation and protected instruction/string equivalence passed. No live
Roblox/Delta test was available. Existing loader/authentication retained.
