ANONHUB 0.40.11 - SACK ROUND TRIP AND TWO CABIN PLATES

Dungeon sack transport refreshes portal references by root/ID, approaches the
actual prompt, waits for availability and makes up to three bounded attempts.
Confirms passage through InDungeon state or the native DungeonDoorTransition
Enter/Exit event. Temporary event listeners are disconnected on every outcome.
Reacquires the active sack after crossing and checks its replicated item count.
Waits for the drill, unloads with DropAll and falls back to confirmed individual
Drop operations. Returns only after count reaches zero, then resumes collection.
Marks matching loot deposited near the drill to avoid immediately collecting
those new loose instances again. Progress identifies exit, unload and return.
Blocked exits are not bypassed; failures preserve remaining sack contents.

Cabin protection now defaults to two SheetMetal plates: opposing left/right
sides of the driver's seat. Selector cycles 2, 4, 6, 8. Existing attached plates
are not removed when lowering the requested count. Native weld confirmation
continues to determine whether placement succeeded.

Validation: portal confirmation/retry/lock/cancellation/listener simulations,
transport and PT/EN UI checks, plate selector, existing regression checks,
Luau compilation and protected-output equivalence. Not tested live in Delta.
