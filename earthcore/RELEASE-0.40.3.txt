ANONHUB 0.40.3
ESP limit configurable from 1 to 200 (60 for fresh settings).
Enabled dungeon/arena/treasure-door categories take priority over nearer objects
by default. Priority button cycles Dungeons / Enemies / Loot / Distance.
Existing category checkboxes and maximum distance remain in effect.
Quick aura, pickaxe and flight arrow buttons start hidden on every load.
Movement > Show quick buttons reveals the optional bar. Drag its labeled handle
with mouse or touch to move all four buttons together. Hiding resets vertical
flight input. Showing the bar does not enable automation.
PT/EN and authentication preserved. Compile, protection equivalence and local
logic/UI tests passed. In-game validation remains necessary.
